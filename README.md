# Prayer All The Time

Prayer All The Time is a comprehensive Android application designed to assist Muslims in their daily religious practices. The app provides a wide range of features to help users stay connected to their faith and perform their religious duties with ease and convenience.

## Features

- **Prayer Times:** Get accurate prayer times based on your location.
- **Qibla Direction:** Find the direction of the Qibla from anywhere in the world.
- **Holy Quran:** Read the Holy Quran with translations and tafsir.
- **Hadith:** Access a collection of authentic Hadith.
- **Dua:** Find and learn duas for various occasions.
- **Ramadan Tracker:** Keep track of your fasts and prayers during Ramadan.
- **Habit Tracker:** Build and maintain good Islamic habits.
- **Wudu Guide:** Learn the steps of performing wudu with illustrations.
- **Tahajjud Guide:** Get guidance on performing the Tahajjud prayer.
- **Charity:** Find and donate to charitable organizations.
- **Family:** Share your progress and connect with your family members.
- **Explore:** Discover new Islamic content and articles.
- **Achievements:** Earn badges and achievements for your progress.
- **Analytics:** Track your prayer and Quran reading habits.
- **Calendar:** View the Islamic calendar and important dates.
- **Settings:** Customize the app to your preferences.
- **Notifications:** Get reminders for prayer times and other important events.

## How it Works

### Prayer Times
The app uses your device's location to calculate accurate prayer times for your city. You can also manually select your location.

### Qibla Direction
The app uses the built-in compass to show you the direction of the Qibla.

### Holy Quran
You can read the Quran in Arabic with translations in multiple languages. The app also provides tafsir for each verse.

### Hadith
The app contains a collection of authentic Hadith from various books of Hadith. You can search for Hadith by keyword or topic.

### Dua
The app has a collection of duas for various occasions, such as before and after meals, entering and leaving the mosque, and more.

## Installation

1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/your-username/prayer-all-the-time.git
   ```
2. Open the project in Android Studio.
3. Build and run the app.

## Technologies Used

- **Kotlin:** The primary programming language for the app.
- **Jetpack Compose:** For building the UI.
- **Hilt:** For dependency injection.
- **Room:** For local database storage.
- **Retrofit:** For making network requests.
- **Google Maps API:** For displaying maps and calculating directions.
